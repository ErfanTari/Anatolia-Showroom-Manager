## A sketch to react to

**Entrance / first view:** give Hit products a clearly visible face. **Live rooms and first-presented sliding faces:** prioritize Prime positioning with a coherent application. **Commercial story:** show suitable commercial products in credible floor/large-area applications. These are proposed guidelines; they do not assign an unconfirmed product to a new commercial tier automatically.

**Display run:** keep a family together; separate warm and neutral branches only where the family needs it; order each branch light to dark. A bookmatch pair is intentional. A second finish can be valuable. An exact repeat with no distinct purpose is the first replacement candidate.

**Missing product:** try a compatible movable slot. If none fits, propose a labeled library sample or a new verified fixture/application. Do not resize a slab visually and treat that as a production specification.

**Fixture movement:** retain the product/face assignments with the frame. Check the plan bounds, route, neighboring objects and operating envelope. The current 1.2 m central keep-out sketch is an illustrative software constraint, **not an approved design or code dimension**.

## Minimum handoff

- Sales/Product: a dated list of product IDs, released variants, SKU, format, finish, thickness, lifecycle and available stock.
- Merch: the reviewed visibility map, required assortment, repeat exceptions, color sequence and named release owner.
- Interior Design: dimensioned plan and reusable supplier fixture models, constraints and approved brand examples.
- Showroom: a numbered photo walk matching slot IDs, actual exceptions, W9F status and kitchen furniture identity.
- Our team: validate imports, turn decisions into versioned rules, compare before/after proposals and measure acceptance.
