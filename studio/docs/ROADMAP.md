## Next increment: confirm the operating pilot

| Owner | Deliverable | Acceptance criterion |
|---|---|---|
| Merch + showroom | Numbered APS walk and reviewed prime map | Every displayed face maps to a slot; front/back and closed sliding positions are confirmed |
| Sales/Product | Released SKU master and alias decisions | No unreviewed development name is counted as a released product; Tuscano variants and stock are known |
| Merch | Required assortment and approved color runs | A person can explain each repeat exception and compare the suggested sequence with physical samples |
| Interior Design | Dimensioned plan and supplier fixture limits | Thickness, load, anchoring, route and movement constraints can be evaluated without guessing |
| Our team | End-to-end rehearsal using one real replacement | Merch releases it, staff track receipt/preparation/install, and evidence updates the correct actual slot |

The current 3D scene is a spatial review reconstruction. It does not certify circulation, accessibility, structural loads, fire requirements or fabrication. Geometry checks use a coarse footprint, room keep-outs and fixture rectangles. Obtain the site's approved constraints from the responsible personnel; do not promote the illustrative dimensions into architectural policy.

## Then: reusable design from an empty showroom

Move the remaining architectural mesh descriptions into a versioned shell/geometry schema. Add new fixture and product registration, structural migrations, slot creation/capacity, reusable GLB assets, fabrication/cut packages, inventory and multi-step task dependencies. Enable a proposed fixture layout with full movement envelopes, then assign variants to its slots. Compare coverage, first-view exposure, family continuity, installation cost and constraints as separate explainable objectives.

The current pilot can move existing eligible frames and swap existing slots. It does not yet generate a new building shell or arbitrary furniture/fixture layouts from scratch. The source schema and shared validator are the starting contract for that increment.

## Then: agentic operations and company systems

Add a product lifecycle/sales feed through a staging importer with stable IDs and effective dates. Convert changes into events; evaluate only affected showrooms; create a proposed refresh and collection tasks. Add customer feedback tied to showroom, slot, variant and visit context, with controlled access to personal data. Add a durable notification queue and explicit delivery receipts before automatic follow-up.

Use an authenticated hosted API and attachment store for multiple staff. Preserve Merch authority and logged manager observations. Connect the future command center to this API/MCP contract rather than reading spreadsheets or renderer code independently.

## Acceptance measures

Track distinct released designs/finishes shown, required-assortment coverage, first-view Hit exposure, justified versus unjustified repeats, measured geometry violations, cost/moves per refresh, overdue work and time from launch decision to verified installation. Keep metric definitions and source periods in the data contract. Image-derived ranks and prototype scores are not substitutes for Sales or Merch's approved measures.
